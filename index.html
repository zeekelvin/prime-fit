Back Care 🩹

For back pain, sciatica, pinched nerve & herniated disc. Gentle, disc-safe training to lose weight & belly fat, build muscle, and strengthen your back & core — without triggering a flare.

⚠️ Important — read first

This section is general fitness information, not medical advice and not a replacement for your doctor or physio. Seek urgent care if you have: loss of bladder/bowel control, numbness around the groin/saddle area, leg weakness that is getting worse, fever with back pain, pain after a fall/accident, or unexplained weight loss. Stop any exercise that causes sharp, shooting pain below the knee, new numbness, or weakness — and log it below.

How's your back today?

Log pain daily. If today is a flare (or pain ≥7), your Today dashboard automatically switches to Gentle Mode.

Your plan: lose fat, build muscle, strong back & core

  • 🎯 Lose weight & belly fat: Daily walking (your safest calorie burner) + protein at every meal + the Meals tab. Belly fat shrinks with overall fat loss — core work makes the muscles under it strong.
  • 💪 Build muscle: 2 gentle strength days/week, neutral spine, light dumbbells/machines, leave 2 reps in reserve. Glutes, back, chest & rows — no heavy spinal loading.
  • 🦴 Strengthen back & core: McGill Big 3 daily (Bird Dog, Modified Curl-Up, Side Plank) — endurance, not max strength, is what protects a disc.
  • 🧘 Go easy rule: Pain ≤3/10 during exercise is OK; pain that shoots down the leg or lingers >24h means you did too much — cut the dose in half next time.

🔥 Flare-day protocol — when it flares up

  1. Don't panic, don't bed-rest all day. Short, frequent gentle walks (5–10 min) usually help more than lying still.
  2. Find your position of relief: lying on your front, on your side with a pillow between knees, or on your back with calves on a chair — use whichever eases leg pain.
  3. Heat or ice: whichever feels better, 15–20 min at a time. Many people prefer heat for stiffness, ice for a fresh flare.
  4. Only the easy Big 3 if they ease symptoms — bird dog & side plank on knees, small range. Skip strength & HIIT today.
  5. Log it above (pain, trigger, what helped) — patterns are how you stop the next flare. Resume normal gentle training when pain is back to your usual baseline, usually in a few days. If it isn't settling, or you have any red-flag symptoms, see your clinician.

Sciatica help

Nerve glides (gentle, never forced), piriformis stretch, walking, and avoiding long sitting. Get up every 30 min — set a reminder.

Pinched nerve help

Keep the neck/spine neutral, avoid looking down at a phone for long stretches, use supported positions, and train the muscles around — not through — the irritated area.

Host it yourself — GitHub + Vercel

This is the static edition of the app: one index.html plus an assets folder, no server or database. To run your own copy:

  1. Put index.html and the assets folder in a GitHub repository.
  2. In Vercel, choose Add New → Project, import that repo — no build settings needed — and deploy.
  3. Every push to GitHub redeploys automatically. Your logs and profile save in each browser you use (they don't sync between devices), so export a backup below before switching phones.
